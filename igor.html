IGORTyler, The Creator

About the Album

"IGOR" is Tyler, The Creator's fifth studio album, released in 2019. Written, produced and arranged entirely by Tyler, it tells the story of a love triangle and a heartbreak through a mix of neo-soul, funk, synth-pop and distorted hip-hop. Introducing his blonde-wigged alter ego Igor, it became his first number-one album on the Billboard 200 and won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album.
